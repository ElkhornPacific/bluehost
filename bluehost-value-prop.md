# Website Manager

> Working draft

## Take website management off your to-do list.

### Meet Website Manager

Your website is important and your time is limited.  Website Manager is your trusted 24/7 website warrior: it watches your site, handles routine fixes, and checks that everything still works.  When something needs your approval or business judgment, it brings you the issue and a clear recommendation. Website Manager runs your website so you can run your business.

### Where it fits in the Bluehost ecosystem

Website Manager is the front door for Bluehost’s website management tools and artificial intelligence (AI) agents. The customer deals with one manager while Bluehost’s specialized products do the work behind the scenes:

- [Managed WordPress](https://www.bluehost.com/help/article/managed-wordpress) and [Bluehost Cloud](https://www.bluehost.com/cloud-hosting) provide hosting, updates, backups, security, and performance tools.
- [AI Store](https://www.bluehost.com/ai-store) manages products, inventory, orders, and other ecommerce work.
- [AI Front Desk](https://www.bluehost.com/ai-front-desk-agent) answers customer questions, captures leads, and books appointments.
- Future agents plug into Website Manager to find problems, make repairs, or improve the site without becoming new products the owner has to learn.

For an ecommerce customer, AI Store works as part of Website Manager rather than as a competing experience. As better agents become available, Bluehost can change what happens behind the scenes without changing the product the customer knows. The promise stays the same: **Website Manager removes website troubleshooting from your to-do list.**

Customers see three simple states:

- **Handled for you:** The work is done and the result has been checked.
- **Needs your decision:** Website Manager has a recommendation but needs the owner’s approval or judgment.
- **Watching for you:** Website Manager is monitoring something important and has not found a problem.

### Why it makes sense

The research supports Website Manager as a product direction worth testing, not as a proven demand case among existing Bluehost customers. The general small-business evidence comes from the May 2026 [Bluehost AI Study](docs/BH_AI_Study_Download.pdf), which surveyed 350 U.S. owners of businesses with 1 to 50 employees and gives a full-sample margin of error of ±5.2 percentage points. The report does not say that respondents were Bluehost customers. What matters is the pattern across the findings.

**Owners want the work done, not another AI category to manage.** “Managing website” ranked sixth of seven broad areas where owners saw AI as relevant, but making site and search engine optimization updates ranked first among specific agent jobs. That difference supports the way Website Manager is framed. It is not an open-ended AI administrator. It has one understandable job: keep the website working and take routine work off the owner’s list.

**Time saved and low trust define the experience.** The study says 78% of owners save time with AI each week, while only 16% had put an agent to work and only 6% highly trust AI with their brand voice. Website Manager shows the work it handled, operates within clear permissions, and leaves customer-facing decisions with the owner. The value is work removed and proof that it was done, not autonomy for its own sake.

**Bluehost’s support structure makes one manager more useful than another specialized tool.** The [troubleshooting guide](https://www.bluehost.com/help/article/bh-general-website-troubleshooting), [WordPress support policy](https://www.bluehost.com/help/article/wordpress-scope-of-support), and [email delivery guide](https://www.bluehost.com/help/article/email-deliverability-problems) split website problems across technical checks, tools, and support handoffs. Customer reports about [intermittent site failures](https://www.reddit.com/r/BlueHost/comments/1t11zpm/hosting_down/) and [contact-form messages being filtered without a clear warning](https://www.reddit.com/r/Wordpress/comments/1pe4r33/bluehost_is_stopping_contact_form_emails_from/) show how failures cross those boundaries or remain hidden. The reports do not establish prevalence, but they support one manager that watches across systems and gives the owner one place to see what is happening.

**AI Store shows that the model fits the Bluehost ecosystem.** [AI Store](https://www.bluehost.com/ai-store) brings monitoring, recommendations, and automated work together for ecommerce customers. Website Manager applies that approach to the entire website and gives current and future agents one customer-facing home. That fit is a product judgment rather than a customer finding, but it makes Website Manager a stronger direction than another stand-alone agent.

### How we know it is working

Measure whether customers trust Website Manager, whether it takes work off their plate, and whether that changes the Bluehost business.

1. **Trusted activation**  
   Percentage of eligible customers who turn it on, allow at least one kind of action, and keep it running after 90 days.

2. **Work completed without troubleshooting**  
   Tasks found, completed, and checked per active site each month without a support contact. Track false alarms, failed checks, reversed changes, and actions taken without permission.

3. **Customer and business results**  
   Compared with a similar group that does not receive Website Manager: reported troubleshooting time, website-related support contacts per 100 sites, renewal rates, and cancellations.

### Expected business impact

Website Manager is designed to support the business in four ways:

- **Renewals:** Customers can see that Bluehost is actively looking after their website. We expect that visibility to give them a stronger reason to renew.
- **Support cost:** Website Manager finds and fixes routine problems earlier. We expect that to reduce repeat support contacts and escalations.
- **Paid upgrades:** Basic monitoring fits within hosting, while automated fixes and advanced management create a reason to upgrade.
- **A place for future agents:** Bluehost can add new capabilities to Website Manager without asking customers to understand another product.
